Write a MATLAB script for an undergraduate EM course (base MATLAB, ASCII characters only) that: (1) models a straight conductor, l = 2 m, I = 1 A in +z, centered at A(0.25, 0.5, 0) m (z from −1 to 1 m), observation point P(1, 1, 0) m, SI units; (2) computes B at P with the closed-form finite-wire formula and, independently, by numerical Biot–Savart integration; (3) prints both vectors and norm(B_num-B_ref)/norm(B_ref), checks the right-hand rule, and stops with a message if the vector error exceeds 0.1%; (4) plots the wire, A, P and B with quiver3, labeled axes with units, a legend and axis equal; (5) uses local functions for each solution and the plot, and comments each equation.
